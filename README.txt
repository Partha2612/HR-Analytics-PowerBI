# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard — Power BI

An interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard built with Microsoft Power BI** to analyze employee demographics, satisfaction, workforce composition, and attrition patterns.

The dashboard transforms HR data into actionable insights that can help organizations understand their workforce, identify employee satisfaction drivers, and analyze attrition trends.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Employee retention and workforce satisfaction are critical challenges for organizations.

This project uses **Power BI, DAX, data modeling, and interactive visualizations** to analyze employee-level HR data across three major analytical areas:

* 👥 **Workforce / Demographics**
* 😊 **Employee Satisfaction**
* 📉 **Employee Attrition**

The dashboard enables users to interactively explore employee characteristics and identify patterns across departments, job roles, salary levels, gender, age groups, and other HR attributes.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze overall employee demographics
* Understand workforce distribution across departments and job roles
* Analyze employee satisfaction and engagement
* Identify factors associated with employee attrition
* Compare attrition across different employee segments
* Analyze salary slabs and job levels
* Provide an interactive HR decision-support dashboard
* Convert raw HR data into meaningful business insights

---

## 📌 Dashboard Pages

The Power BI report contains three primary analytical sections.

### 1. 👥 Workforce / Demographics

The workforce analysis provides an overview of the employee population.

Key analysis includes:

* Total number of employees
* Average employee age
* Average monthly rate
* Average relationship satisfaction
* Average years at company
* Department-wise employee distribution
* Gender distribution
* Education field
* Job roles
* Job levels
* Performance ratings
* Education levels
* Hourly rate
* Work-life balance

Interactive filters allow users to drill down into specific employee segments.

---

### 2. 😊 Employee Satisfaction

The satisfaction dashboard focuses on employee experience and workplace engagement.

Key variables analyzed include:

* Job Satisfaction
* Job Involvement
* Work-Life Balance
* Environment Satisfaction
* Relationship Satisfaction
* Department
* Gender
* Marital Status
* Employee demographics

The dashboard helps identify differences in satisfaction levels across employee groups and organizational functions.

---

### 3. 📉 Employee Attrition

The attrition analysis focuses on employee turnover and potential retention risks.

Key analysis includes:

* Total attrition
* Attrition rate / margin
* Attrition by department
* Attrition by job role
* Attrition by job level
* Attrition by gender
* Attrition by age group
* Attrition by salary slab
* Attrition by business travel
* Percent salary hike
* Employee-level attrition patterns

This section can help HR teams identify employee segments with comparatively higher attrition.
